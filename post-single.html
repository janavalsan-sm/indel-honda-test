Indel Automotive, a subsidiary of Indel Corporation, represents some of the leading automobile brands across Kerala. The company is committed to expanding its presence while consistently delivering exceptional customer experiences characterized by warmth, friendliness, and a sense of unity.

Indel Automotive, a proud division of Indel Corporation, stands as a trusted name in the automobile industry, representing some of the world’s most renowned automobile brands across the beautiful state of Kerala. With a legacy rooted in excellence and innovation, we are committed to delivering superior services and unforgettable experiences to our valued customers.

Our Vision

Our vision is to provide automotive buyers & users with an experience that is extremely satisfying and gives them complete peace of mind.

Our Mission

To provide our customers with a comprehensive one stop solution for all their personal mobility needs by having competent, well trained and motivated staff that is keen to delivering top class service.

Integrity

Integrity in everything we do and to be the heart of every single transaction and relationship.A framework of trust enables the smooth and transparent conduct of all business operations and is the bedrock on which institutions are built. It is something we practice everyday.

Respect & Team work

Respect the contribution of team members and encourage collaboration in every action.

Passion to excell

To be the best at all times . Excellence is not a destination but a never ending journey. For us, every milestone is a new benchmark. Every challenge we face is an opportunity to excel. Every achievement is a step to greater heights.
